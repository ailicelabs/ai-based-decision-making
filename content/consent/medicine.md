## Taking part in this study

This activity is also part of a research study on how AI assistants influence people's decisions, led by Christian Salvatore (IUSS Pavia).

**Anonymous data.** If you agree, your conversation with the assistant is stored for research. We do not collect your name, email or anything else that identifies you, so please do not write personal information in the chat. Your team's answers are collected at team level. The data are used only for research and may appear, in anonymous and aggregate form, in scientific publications.

**The assistant.** The assistant runs on an external AI service (OpenAI), which receives your messages in order to reply.

**About the study.** Some aspects of the study cannot be explained in advance, because knowing them would change the results. They will be explained at the end of the activity.

**Your choice.** Taking part in the research is voluntary. If you do not agree, you can still do the activity, and your conversation will not be stored. Because the data are anonymous, they cannot be withdrawn after the activity.
