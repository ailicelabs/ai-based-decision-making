## Taking part in this study

This activity is also part of a research study by [research group, IUSS Pavia] on how AI assistants influence people's decisions.

**What we collect.** If you agree, we store your conversation with the assistant. We do not collect your name, email or any other information that identifies you. Please do not write personal information in the chat.

**Your team's answers.** The answers your team submits in the panel are collected at team level, anonymously.

**How the data are used.** The data are analysed in anonymous and aggregate form for research purposes and may be published in scientific papers. Data controller: [institution]. Privacy notice: [link]. Contact: [email].

**About the study.** Some aspects of the study cannot be explained in advance, because knowing them would change the results. They will be fully explained at the end of the activity.

**Your choice.** Taking part in the research is voluntary. If you do not agree, you can still do the activity: your conversation will not be stored.

**Withdrawal.** Because the data are anonymous and cannot be linked to you, it is not possible to withdraw them after the activity.
